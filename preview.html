Goods & Services Tax (GST) Guide

Complete GST Knowledge & Compliance Portal

Learn everything about GST registration limits, regular vs composition schemes, monthly returns (GSTR-1, 3B), e-invoicing, and Input Tax Credit (ITC) reconciliation.

Apply for GST Registration (₹999*) File GST Returns (₹499*/mo) GST Help on WhatsApp

Turnover Threshold Limits

  • • Goods: ₹40 Lakhs/year (₹20L in Special Category States)
  • • Services: ₹20 Lakhs/year (₹10L in Special Category States)
  • • Inter-state: Mandatory regardless of turnover limit

Mandatory GST Triggers

  • • E-Commerce Sellers (Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho)
  • • Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM) taxpayers
  • • Non-Resident / Casual Taxable Entities
  • • Input Service Distributors (ISD)

Composition Scheme

  • • Turnover limit up to ₹1.5 Crore (₹75L in Special States)
  • • 1% flat tax for Traders/Manufacturers, 5% for Restaurants
  • • No monthly GSTR-1/3B; only quarterly CMP-08

Regular Scheme vs Composition Scheme Comparison

Feature Regular GST Scheme Composition Scheme
Turnover Eligibility Any turnover Up to ₹1.5 Cr (Goods), ₹50L (Services)
Tax Collection from Buyer Yes (Can issue Tax Invoice) No (Must issue Bill of Supply)
Input Tax Credit (ITC) Yes (Full ITC allowed on purchases) No ITC available
Return Frequency Monthly (GSTR-1 & 3B) or QRMP Quarterly (CMP-08) + Annual GSTR-4
Interstate Supply Allowed without restriction Not allowed (Intrastate only)

Which ITR Form Should You File?

Select the right form based on your income sources to prevent defective return notices.

ITR-1 (Sahaj)
Salaried & Pensioners

For resident individuals with total income up to ₹50 Lakhs from Salary, 1 House Property, and Other Sources (Interest/Dividend).

ITR-2
Capital Gains & Multi-Property

For individuals & HUFs having income from Capital Gains (Shares, Mutual Funds, Crypto, Real Estate) or multiple house properties.

ITR-3
Business & Profession (Books)

For individuals and HUFs having income from proprietary business or profession maintaining regular books of accounts.

ITR-4 (Sugam)
Presumptive Business / Freelance

For small businesses under Section 44AD (6%/8% profit) and professionals/freelancers under Section 44ADA (50% profit).

Key Deductions Checklist (Old Tax Regime)

Section 80C (Max ₹1.5 Lakhs)

EPF, PPF, ELSS Mutual Funds, Life Insurance Premium, Children's Tuition Fees, Principal Home Loan repayment.

Section 80D (Health Insurance)

Up to ₹25,000 for self & family + up to ₹50,000 for senior citizen parents (Total up to ₹75k-1L).

Section 24(b) (Home Loan Interest)

Deduction up to ₹2,00,000 for interest paid on self-occupied residential property loan.

Section 80CCD(1B) (NPS Extra ₹50k)

Additional deduction of up to ₹50,000 for voluntary contribution to National Pension System.

HRA Exemption (House Rent)

Exemption calculated based on rent paid, salary component, and city of residence.

Section 80TTA / 80TTB (Interest)

Up to ₹10,000 savings interest for general, up to ₹50,000 FD/Savings interest for senior citizens.
